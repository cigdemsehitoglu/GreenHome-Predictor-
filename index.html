About This Model

How it works

A multiple linear regression predicts monthly electricity use from home size, number of people, outdoor temperature, insulation quality and heating type.

Key Insights

Larger homes and more occupants increase consumption almost linearly. Poor insulation and electric heating raise the estimate significantly, especially in cold months.

Interactive Use

Move the sliders and change the dropdowns, then click Calculate Prediction. Your predicted home appears as a highlighted point on the scatter plot.

Reproducibility

All data points are generated with a fixed seed. The regression formula is hard-coded so the page works offline and the results stay identical every time.
